2. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

We do not create user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone, address, or precise location.

The game stores locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs:

  • Level progress and unlocked puzzles
  • Candies (in-game currency) balance
  • Game settings (sound, etc.)

This data never leaves your device except as described for advertising.

3. Advertising - Google AdMob

Unicorn Puzzle displays ads using Google AdMob via the Gley Ads mediation plugin. This is how the game remains free.

Types of ads:

  • Banner Ad: Persistent banner at the bottom of the screen
  • Interstitial Ad: Shown after a puzzle is completed, only after you tap Next/Replay/Menu, max once per 3 minutes, not before level 4
  • Rewarded Ad: Optional - watch a full video to earn +20 Candies. Reward is granted only after AdMob confirms completion.

Child-Directed Configuration:

  • TagForChildDirectedTreatment = true
  • TagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true (via Google UMP)
  • MaxAdContentRating = G (General audiences only)
  • Non-personalized ads only - no behavioral tracking

Google AdMob may automatically collect for ad delivery and abuse prevention:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Advertising ID (if available) - handled in a child-directed, non-tracking manner
  • IP address (may be used to infer approximate city/country, then truncated)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completions)
  • App package name and app instance identifiers

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & Children

4. Consent and GDPR / EEA / UK

We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), included with Google Mobile Ads SDK 11.6, to handle consent.

  • Consent information is requested on every app launch
  • No ads are requested until UMP returns CanRequestAds() = true
  • Errors never bypass consent

Because the app is tagged as directed to children / under age of consent, Google does NOT show the standard personalized-ads consent form to children. Ads remain non-personalized regardless of consent choices. This is expected behavior.

Parents in the EEA, UK and Switzerland can use the in-game Settings > Privacy Options button to review choices where Google requires it.

5. Children's Privacy

Unicorn Puzzle is directed to children under 13 and is part of Google Play's Designed for Families. We comply with applicable children's privacy requirements including the principles of COPPA and GDPR-K.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children directly
  • All ads are G-rated and non-personalized
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access is requested
  • Interstitials are not shown during gameplay, only after completion on user action

If you are a parent/guardian and believe data was collected in error, contact us at the email above for deletion.
